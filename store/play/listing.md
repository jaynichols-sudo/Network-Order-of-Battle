# Google Play listing

Paste these into Play Console > Grow > Store presence > Main store listing.

## App name (30 max)

Bearings: Your Network Map

## Short description (80 max)

See who needs you this week, who changed jobs, and who you know at every company.

## Full description (4000 max)

Bearings turns your LinkedIn export into a private, living map of the people you know, so you always know where you stand and who to reach out to.

KNOW WHO NEEDS YOU
• Today opens on the people worth your time this week, each with one plain reason and one tap to reply, congratulate or check in
• See who wrote last and is waiting on your reply
• Spot good relationships that are going cold
• Keep-in-touch circles: monthly, quarterly or yearly, with a nudge when someone is overdue
• Catch up on new connections and job changes with a quick swipe

SEE EVERY COMPANY
• Who you know at each company, agency or command, at every level from executive to staff
• Coverage gaps on your watchlist, like no director-level contact yet
• Ways in: the best paths into any company, from people there now, people who used to be, and people close to you in the same field
• Everyone sorted by industry, with a tap to fix anything

EVENTS AND TRAVEL
• Event mode: load the attendee list to see who you know before you go, note who you met, and work through a follow-up list afterward
• Trips: add a trip and see who you know within 25 to 100 miles, with a ready-made "I'll be in town" message
• Birthdays this week, from your contacts

EXPLORE YOUR NETWORK
• The compass: your whole network on one screen, by sector and closeness
• Clusters and a seniority scope
• Plain-English search like "navy o-5 and up in cyber" or "close contacts at Duke Energy"
• An optional federal and military view with branch, rank, agency and segment

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account and no Bearings server. Your network stays on your phone. Contacts and location are read on your phone and never uploaded. No ads, no tracking. Back up your notes to a file whenever you like.

To start, request your data from LinkedIn (Settings, Data privacy, Get a copy of your data) and open the zip in Bearings. You can also start from your phone's contacts while you wait for LinkedIn's email.
