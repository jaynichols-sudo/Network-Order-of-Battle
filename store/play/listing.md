## App name (30 max)

Northfix: Your Network Map

## Short description (80 max)

See who needs you this week and your warmest way into every agency and bid.

## Full description (4000 max, this is about 3,700)

Northfix turns your LinkedIn export into a private map of the people you know. See who needs you this week, who you know at every agency and company, and your warmest way into every bid.

KNOW WHO NEEDS YOU
• Today opens on the people worth your time this week, each with one plain reason and one tap to reply, congratulate or check in
• See who wrote last and is waiting on your reply
• Spot good relationships that are going cold
• Keep-in-touch circles: monthly, quarterly or yearly, with a nudge when someone is overdue
• Catch up on new connections and job changes with a quick swipe

WIN THE PURSUIT
• Pursuits: map who decides each bid (sponsor, program manager, contracting officer, mission owner), plus the incumbent and your teaming partners
• Pipeline: totals by stage, a win chance for each pursuit, and your won and lost history
• Cold seats: a warning when a decision maker has gone quiet and the due date is close
• Call plans written on your phone before you talk to someone in a seat
• SAM.gov notices matched to who you know at the buying office, with your own free key
• Recompetes: contracts ending in the next one to two years at the agencies you follow, from USASpending.gov, with the incumbent and who you know on both sides
• Teaming partners: the primes and subs that win at a target agency, and who you know at each

SEE EVERY AGENCY AND COMPANY
• Who you know at each company, agency or command, at every level from executive to staff
• The federal map: departments, agencies and commands, with the ones you have no one at called out
• Coverage gaps on your watchlist, like no director-level contact yet
• Org charts with the empty seats called out
• Ways in: your best paths into any company, from people there now, people who used to be, and people close to you in the same field

EVENTS AND TRAVEL
• Event mode: load the attendee list to see who you know and who is new, note who you met, and work the follow-up list afterward
• Trips: add a trip and see who you know nearby, with a ready-made "I'll be in town" message
• Arrival check: open Northfix in a new city and see who you know there
• Birthdays this week, from your contacts

NORTHFIX TEAM
• Pool who your whole team knows: ways in through every teammate, the team federal map, team pursuits and the team watchlist
• End-to-end encrypted: each share is locked on your phone with a key that lives only in your invite link, so the team server can't read it
• Invite by link, with roles and admin controls. Nobody sees anyone's notes or messages

EXPLORE YOUR NETWORK
• The compass: your whole network on one screen, by sector and closeness
• Plain-English search like "navy o-5 and up in cyber"
• An optional federal and military view with branch, rank, agency and segment
• Bring in your Facebook, Instagram, Snapchat and TikTok downloads to see everyone you know in one place
• Fill in work email, phone and city from your own ZoomInfo or Seamless.AI account, or from a file

NORTHFIX PRO
The map, Today, search and reminders are free. Northfix Pro adds event mode, ways in, Team, the arrival check, and ZoomInfo and Seamless.AI. Choose monthly, yearly or a one-time lifetime purchase. Subscriptions renew until you cancel them in Google Play.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No sign-up. Your network stays on your phone. Contacts and location are read on your phone and never uploaded. Team shares are encrypted before they leave. No ads, no tracking.

To start, request your data from LinkedIn (Settings, Data privacy, Get a copy of your data) and open the zip in Northfix. You can start from your phone's contacts or the sample network while you wait for LinkedIn's email.

## Content rating (Policy > App content > Content rating)

Category: **Utility, Productivity, Communication or Other**. Answer **No** to every question: no violence, sexual content, profanity, drugs, gambling or simulated gambling. Two to read carefully:

- **Does the app let users interact or exchange content?** No. Team shares are encrypted summaries between invited teammates, with no chat, posts or public profiles. If you'd rather be cautious, answer Yes; it adds "Users interact" but doesn't change the rating.
- **Does the app share the user's current location with other users?** No. Location is used on the phone only.
- **Does the app allow purchases of digital goods?** Yes (Northfix Pro).

Expected result: Everyone (IARC 3+, ESRB Everyone), with "In-app purchases".
